Approach as one timeline: 10 concepts

Each concept replaces the two-column sketch plus list with a single timeline unit. All existing content is kept: the headline, the three numbered steps, their titles and copy, the three illustration captions, and the three sketches (notes and focus frame, connected parts, support loop), redrawn per step in amber, blue and green.

01

Drawn rail

Closest to the current site. A horizontal rail draws from step to step and each sketch assembles as the line reaches it. Calm, clear, and easy to build from today's markup.

Approach as one animation: 4 concepts

Each concept is one continuous animated scene that plays through all three steps. The timeline underneath is its playhead: press pause, drag it to scrub, or click a step to jump to it. The caption, title and copy for each step change as the scene moves. All the existing content is included. (The earlier static concepts are in index.html.)

A

One sketch, three acts

The current illustrations joined into one story. Scattered notes with question marks gather inside a focus frame. Then they straighten into connected building blocks, and finally merge into one system with a support loop running around it. Closest to the brand's existing drawing style.

B

Assembly line

The timeline as a conveyor belt with three stations. A jumble of shapes is inspected and sorted, assembled into one product by two arms, then stamped and handed over. A return arrow runs from the last station back to the first: support continues.

C

Live editor

A code editor plays a short session. Questions are typed into a brief and scope is agreed, then the build file is written and the parts connect. Finally a terminal hands everything over and a heartbeat keeps running. The most “software studio” of the four.

D

Pieces fall into place

Loose pieces drift with question marks until the outline of the whole is agreed. Then each piece flies into its slot, the grid fuses into one block, and a new slot opens beside it for the next piece. The simplest and most abstract of the four.

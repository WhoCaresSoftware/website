# Work cover stories

Selected-work covers are conceptual editorial illustrations, not client
screenshots or game assets. Original Hex Mythica gallery artwork retains its
separate attribution.

## Visual language

Each project has its own visual story, composed for its frame rather than cropped
from a universal portrait. Use warm monochrome materials, Deep Teal overprints,
decisive photographic planes and a fine amber route. Aigistos is the visual
benchmark.

Preserve the six-cover overview: large Aigistos, tall Hex Mythica, two landscape
covers and two panoramic workflow covers. Compact compositions replace panoramas
on smaller frames; the tablet Hex tile receives a horizontal companion. Keep
HTML titles readable over quiet lower areas.

## Composition stories

| Subject | Story | Visual cue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Worksite operations | A worksite connected to its operational overview. | Construction materials, crane, site plan and connected devices. |
| Hex Mythica | The world inside the cards becomes a place to explore. | An amber journey through a stone arch into a mountain valley. |
| Private infrastructure | Applications within an organizational boundary. | Infrastructure and its user in an enclosed architectural interior. |
| Campaign imagery | One source product in multiple campaign contexts. | The same bottle, cap and label in coastal, architectural and botanical settings. |
| Engineering workflow | A brief becomes a tested, reviewed software change. | Plan, code and review artifacts across a horizontal workbench. |
| Business operations | Incoming material becomes review-ready work. | Contract and ledger workflows converging at human review. |

## Asset rules

- Use Aigistos as a style reference, not a subject to copy.
- Compose panoramic, landscape, tall and compact companions for their intended
  frames. Select them through native picture sources and shared media conditions.
- The two-column work layout starts at 1200px; navigation uses its independent
  1000px breakpoint.
- Never add fictional project claims, confidential records or text resembling
  client data. Localized HTML carries the project facts.
- Retain required attribution and licenses. Review embedded asset metadata before
  publication; keep production notes and review captures outside the repository.

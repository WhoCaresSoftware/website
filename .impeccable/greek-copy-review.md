# Greek wording review

Reviewed 24 October 2026. This is editorial research, not public site content.

## References

- https://www.intel-soft.gr/custom-software — concrete business tasks and a clear enquiry prompt.
- https://streamcode.gr/ — direct collaboration and plain explanations of the work.
- https://customlab.gr/custom-software-solutions-development/ — explains handover and support, though its prose is more formal.
- https://byte12.com/custom-software/ — useful customer questions about support; technical terminology needs explaining.
- https://www.softaware.gr/ — familiar wording such as “λογισμικό φτιαγμένο στα μέτρα σας”.
- https://agileturtles.gr/el — recognisable business problems, but English-heavy wording; only limited page content was extractable.

## Editorial choices for human review

Friendly professional plural (“σας”), active verbs and concrete explanations.
Homepage copy describes business needs before technical disciplines. Project
details explain unfamiliar terms; CVs retain technical roles and credentials.
No new prices, delivery times, support guarantees or measured outcomes were added.

Key revised wording:

- “Πώς μπορούμε να βοηθήσουμε”
- “Αξιόπιστες υποδομές”
- “Πρώτα ακούμε. Μετά βρίσκουμε τη σωστή λύση.”
- “Καταλαβαίνουμε τι χρειάζεστε”
- “Φτιάχνουμε τη λύση μαζί σας”
- “Σκεφτόμαστε και τη συνέχεια”
- “Τι θα έκανε τη δουλειά σας πιο εύκολη;”
- “Ας συζητήσουμε τι χρειάζεστε”

The composer says explicitly that it prepares a message and hands off to an email
app. It does not present itself as a send form. Editable greetings remain plain
text. All six founder-name mentions on the homepage are orchid, underlined links
opening the existing CV dialog, including after language changes. The open CV’s
own name and biography remain reading content rather than links to themselves.

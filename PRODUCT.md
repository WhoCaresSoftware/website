# Product

## Purpose and audience

Who Cares Software is an independent software studio. The website helps business
owners, operations leads and technical leaders understand the studio's services,
explore selected work and start a conversation, even without a finished brief.

Use plain language at entry, with concrete engineering detail where useful.
The voice is dry, direct and confident, and it leans into the name: short
sentences, concrete nouns, a little wit, no agency filler. Wit never replaces or
stretches a fact. Greek keeps the same tone in the formal plural, as its own
copy rather than a literal translation.
Services cover workflow automation, custom software and cloud/platform engineering.

## Website

- Static HTML, CSS and JavaScript, built with Vite and hosted on GitHub Pages.
- Complete English and Greek content, with light and dark appearances.
- Selected-work pages explain the problem, delivered scope and supported outcomes.
- Contact hands off to the visitor's email or sharing application. The website
  does not send messages, upload attachments or store enquiries on a server.
- Without JavaScript, English content, navigation and contact links remain usable.

## Identity

Use the lowercase whocares wordmark, WC symbol and Deep Teal identity supplied by
`@whocaressoftware/brand`. The website owns spacing, typography and sizing; theme
colors and identity artwork come from the pinned brand package.

## Content standards

- Describe delivered work accurately and distinguish illustrations from real interfaces.
- Publish only material cleared for public use; protect confidential client details.
- Use synthetic data in demonstrations, never client records.
- Do not invent metrics, quotations, endorsements or contractual guarantees.
- Preserve third-party credits and licenses.
- Keep private interviews, agreements and internal review records outside this repository.

## Visitor preferences

On first visit, a Greek primary browser language selects Greek; other languages
select English. Appearance follows the system preference. Visitors can change
language and appearance, with choices stored locally when storage is available.
Denied storage must not prevent changes for the current session.

## Principles

- Help visitors assess fit before asking them to contact us.
- Explain practical problems and deliverables.
- Show accountable people and attributable work.
- Make contact predictable and welcome incomplete briefs.
- Keep promises grounded in actual delivery and support practices.

Selected work — seven display concepts

Interactive mockups for showing whocares projects. They use the site's dark palette and the real project copy. Hover, scroll, click and drag in each one. Each concept explains when it works best.

01

Bento showcase

Asymmetric tiles with the headline project at double size, mixed with a stat tile. Shows everything at once and feels editorial. Best when there's one project to feature.

Built for teams that ship.

02

Hover index

A large typographic list; a preview card follows the cursor over each row. Confident and fast to scan, and it scales to many projects. On touch screens the preview becomes a tap.

Index of work
    03

    Sticky stack

    Scroll inside the frame: each project card pins and the next one slides over it, leaving a stacked edge behind. Gives every project a full moment, like turning case-study pages.

    04

    Spotlight

    A project list beside one large stage that auto-advances every five seconds; the progress line shows the timer. Click any project to jump. Compact, and it explains each project in depth.

      05

      Gallery rail

      Wide slabs on a horizontal rail you can drag or scroll, with oversized outline numbers and a progress line. Natural on phones, and roomy for images.

      06

      3D deck

      Projects fanned in perspective like a hand of cards. Click a card, or use ← →, to bring it to the front. Playful and memorable; best with a handful of projects.

      Click a card · ← → keys

      07

      Blueprint board

      Projects pinned as drawing sheets on a grid board, linked by animated dashed lines. Extends the site's architectural-drawing style and the folder metaphor. Most on-brand, least conventional.

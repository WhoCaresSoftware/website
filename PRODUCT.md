## Users

## Product Purpose

Help prospective clients understand Who Cares Software, assess fit and credibility,
and start a relevant conversation. Useful enquiries and substantive conversations
matter more than raw clicks or scrolling.

## Positioning

Independent studio led by Mike and XRS, both co-founders and platform engineers,
with a wider collective of trusted specialists. Practical AI adoption, automation,
custom software and cloud/platform engineering within long-term partnerships.
Understand the problem, question assumptions and recommend what is worth building.
Price projects rather than developer headcount; scope uncertain work before
promising an implementation price.

## Visitor preferences

Selected work also includes Private LLM Gateway, Brand Image Pipelines, and AI
Workflows & Agents. The founder confirms these were built for clients and are used
internally in production. Describe private models whose inputs are excluded from
training, brand-asset-aware image workflows, engineering agents across the SDLC,

The website supports complete English and Greek copy. On first visit, only a
Greek primary browser language (`el`, including `el-GR` and `el-CY`) selects Greek;
all other primary languages select English. A compact EN / ΕΛ button switches
language; a sun/moon icon button switches appearance, defaulting to the system's
dark preference and light otherwise. Manual choices are stored locally; denied
storage still permits session choices. A segmented EN / ΕΛ radio control exposes
the selected language directly; there is no manual reset action. Without JavaScript, the English page
and native contact/navigation links remain usable with the system palette.
Theme owns the website's light and dark color mappings as a generated adapter;
brand owns the rationale and identity assets. Website consumes those outputs.

- Show accountable people and attributable work.
- Make contact predictable and welcome incomplete briefs.
